# Booking Help

## How to book an appointment
Browse the Salons page, open a salon, and pick one of its services. Choose a date to see the available time slots, select a free slot, and confirm. You need to be logged in to book.

## Opening hours and time slots
Appointments are booked in one-hour slots between 9:00 AM and 9:00 PM. All times are shown in India Standard Time (IST).

## Fully booked slots
Each service can only be booked by one customer per time slot. If a slot shows as booked, choose another time or another date.

## Booking confirmation
After you book, we send a confirmation email to the address on your account with the salon, service, date and time.

## Viewing your bookings
Log in and open My Bookings to see the appointments you have booked.
