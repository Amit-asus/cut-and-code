# Account Help

## Creating an account
Choose Login, then Register, and sign up with your email address and a password. New accounts are customer accounts, which can browse salons and make bookings.

## Logging in
Log in with the email address and password you registered with. You stay logged in for up to one hour, after which you need to log in again.

## Viewing and updating your profile
Open your profile from the top right of the site to see your email address, account type and the date you joined. On your profile you can update your avatar and switch between light and dark mode.

## Staff and admin accounts
Salon staff and administrators have separate accounts created by an administrator. They can add salons, services and salon photos.
