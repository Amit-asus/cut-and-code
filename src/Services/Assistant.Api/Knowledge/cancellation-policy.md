# Cancellation Policy

## How to cancel a booking
You can cancel a booking yourself at any time. Log in, open My Bookings, find the appointment and choose Cancel. The time slot is released straight away, so another customer can book it.

## Cancellation fees
There is no cancellation fee. We do not take payment online, so there is nothing to refund when you cancel.

## Rescheduling
Bookings cannot be moved to a new time directly. To reschedule, cancel your existing booking and then book the new time you want.

## Missed appointments
If you can't make it, please cancel as early as possible so the salon can offer the slot to someone else.
